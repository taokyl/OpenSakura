Changes made to the source animations
-------------------------------------

- one animation per file; skeleton only (mesh, skin, materials removed);
  resampled at 30 fps; leaf/helper joints, scale and constant channels dropped
- standing clips: body turned to face forward, feet moved to a narrow stance
  with leg IK (knee bend kept), clenched fists relaxed
- KayKit clips: resting arms lowered toward the body; legs re-posed for her
  proportions (feet planted flat under the hips, pelvis level, knees softly bent)
- hand-keyed overlays layered on the source motion as described above
